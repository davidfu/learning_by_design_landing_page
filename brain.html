Four layers, built in this order

Most teams start at layer four. That is why the agent works in the demo and drifts in month two.

01 · Context

What is true here?

The shared truth your best person carries: ICP, offers, proof, voice.

02 · Skills

How do we do this?

Reusable procedures: research an account, brief a meeting, scope a feature.

03 · Policies

What are the limits?

What is allowed, who approves, and what always gets logged.

04 · Agents

Who does it, and when?

On a schedule, in a channel, on real work, with a human on the gate.

Without a brain
  • Knowledge lives in individual chat histories
  • The ICP is described four ways in four tools
  • Output quality tracks whoever wrote the prompt
With a brain
  • One source of truth every agent reads before it acts
  • The ICP moves once and everything downstream follows
  • Every correction becomes a permanent rule

Agents draft. Humans send. Approval gates on anything outbound, least privilege by default, and a named person on review.
