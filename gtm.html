Why first sales hires stall

Your first salesperson usually doesn't fail on talent. They fail on setup.

You've found early PMF through founder-led sales. Now you're hiring someone to scale it, but everything they'd need to do that still lives in your head and your inbox.

The CRM is a graveyard.

Deals sit in spreadsheets, DMs, and three half-set-up tools. Nobody trusts the data, so nobody uses it.

The cost: your new hire's first month goes to data entry.

There's no real target list.

You know your ICP when you see it, but it's never been turned into a list of accounts and contacts someone else can work.

The cost: generic outreach to the wrong people.

Outbound doesn't scale past you.

Your best emails are personal because you wrote them. Mass sequences get ~25% opens and zero replies.

The cost: a salary with no pipeline behind it.

Before you book

Questions founders ask us first.

We're not edtech. Is this still for us?

Yes. Edtech is where we have the deepest network, but the engine is the same for any B2B or enterprise seller. Chestnut, for example, is an a16z-backed insurtech company.

Should we hire the rep first or build the system first?

Ideally the system is ready before they start, so the ramp-up quarter disappears. If you've already hired, we build around them. At Chestnut the AE had just joined.

Which CRM and tools do you use?

Whatever fits your stage. Often HubSpot or a similar CRM, with Clay, Apollo, or Instantly for data and sequencing, and Claude-powered agents for research and drafting. We don't resell software.

Isn't cold email dead?

Generic cold email is. We pair targeted lists with research-backed, personal outreach, and blend it with warm intros, LinkedIn, and events. Willow went from no replies to 5+ high-value meetings a week.

How long does it take?

The core build (CRM, list, first agents) takes weeks, not quarters. We scope it on our first call based on your stack and hiring timeline.

Who actually does the work?

David leads GTM strategy and RevOps. Vedansh and our build team ship the agents and integrations. You work with the people doing the work, not an account manager.

Fit

Is this you?

You're a great fit if:

  • You sell B2B (or B2B2G) and have early PMF: paying customers, renewals, or high-intent pilots.
  • You understand your ICP and offer well enough to explain why people buy.
  • You're hiring, or about to hire, your first non-founder AE or SDR.
  • Or you're at Series A and your sales tools and data need to become one system.

Probably not yet if:

  • You're pre-revenue and still finding your first customers.
  • You already run a mature sales org with a fully staffed RevOps team.
